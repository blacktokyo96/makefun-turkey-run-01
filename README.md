**Wishbone Getaway** — Pip the turkey vs. Farmer Grimsby, one day before Thanksgiving. A 2.5D three.js runner-with-stealth: Pip auto-runs from the pumpkin patch through the red barn and Grimsby's kitchen to the back door and the whispering woods, chased by the farmer with his carving knives. Dodge fences, pumpkins, mud, rolling barrels and gravy slicks; jump, flap and glide, dash, hide in haystacks and laundry baskets, toss corn decoys, dodge telegraphed knife throws, oven blasts and sliding carving-station knives. Score for distance, corn, close shaves, sneaky escapes and golden wishbones — escape with feathers to spare for the best rank.

Controls: W/S or ↑/↓ dodge · D/→ sprint · A/← ease off · SPACE jump, again to flap · SHIFT dash · E hide/sneak out · CLICK corn decoy · P pause · R retry · ESC title.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests, everything it loads lives under `game/`.

Setiap kali game/ atau screenshots/ berubah, jalankan node tools/make-dossier.ts.
